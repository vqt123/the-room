The tape

Five scenes are generated from your one photo on a Comfy Dev Platform serverless endpoint. Then a private custom node inside that endpoint, a compiled binary, turns them into a VHS tape: 4:3 crop, camcorder drift, chroma bleed, tape grain, scanlines, a rolling tracking band, head-switching noise along the bottom, the date stamp advancing scene to scene, snow between the shots, hiss and mains hum underneath. About a minute when the worker is warm.
